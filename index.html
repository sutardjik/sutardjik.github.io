momohana ↗

What happens when restraint becomes the foundation of form?
momohana is a spatial study of a design philosophy grounded in intention and restraint, and shaped through softness and structure. Through the intersection of fashion, surrealism, and spatial composition, it explores forms shaped by restraint and quiet control. This space articulates how transient experiences can be held, translated, and integrated without losing their softness or ambiguity.
The mineral softness of peach moonstone and the quiet ephemerality of peach blossoms inspire a visual language defined by luminosity, atmosphere, and muted warmth. Soft tonal gradients, diffused light, negative space, and suggestive silhouettes articulate this sensibility.

.calm ↗

How would the web feel if it prioritized perception over performance?
.calm (“dot calm”) is a visual archive of personal aesthetic memory, created to counteract the hyperstimulated logic of conventional platforms. Inspired by the philosophy of the slow web, this space favors presence over performance. The archive surfaces quiet tensions between the constructed and the imagined—where realism meets escapism, and conscious curation intersects with intuitive, subconscious attraction. Interface minimalism and attention-conscious design cultivate a contemplative experience across visual culture and contemporary digital practice. This deliberate approach invites exploration and reflection beyond trends, algorithms, or commercial constraints.

I’m Karen, a self-taught designer from San Francisco. I turn human complexity into digital experiences that bring clarity, intention, and focus.

I combine Computer Science (B.E. 2024) with philosophical thinking and applied psychology to create ethically conscious, adaptable digital spaces. This foundation helps me navigate ambiguity and create meaningful outcomes. I focus on bridging design and human behavior, guided by a curiosity about systems and a belief in human potential.

When I’m not dreaming up designs, you’ll find me exploring fine art museums, immersing myself in art pop, or meditating on critical theory in quiet green spaces.
