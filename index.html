.calm ↗

How would the web feel if it prioritized perception over performance?
.calm (“dot calm”) is a visual archive of personal aesthetic memory, created to counteract the hyperstimulated logic of conventional platforms. Inspired by the philosophy of the slow web, this space favors presence over performance. The archive surfaces quiet tensions between the constructed and the imagined—where realism meets escapism, and conscious curation intersects with intuitive, subconscious attraction. Interface minimalism and attention-conscious design cultivate a contemplative experience across visual culture, design, and digital practice. This deliberate approach invites exploration and reflection beyond trends, algorithms, or commercial constraints.

Welcome to my corner of the web! ❊

I’m Karen, a self-taught designer from San Francisco. I turn human complexity into digital experiences that bring clarity, intention, and focus.

I combine Computer Science (B.E. 2024) with philosophy and applied psychological concepts to design ethically conscious, adaptable digital spaces. This foundation helps me navigate ambiguity and create meaningful outcomes. I focus on bridging design and human behavior, guided by curiosity, a belief in human potential, and a commitment to fostering genuine connection.

When I’m not dreaming up designs, you’ll find me exploring fine art museums, immersing myself in art pop, or meditating on critical theory in quiet green spaces.
